## ⭐ The STAR Method (Quick Reference)

| Step | What to Do |
|------|-----------|
| **Situation** | Set the scene, give context (2 sentences max) |
| **Task** | What was YOUR responsibility? |
| **Action** | What did YOU specifically do? (Use "I", not "we") |
| **Result** | What was the outcome? Use numbers wherever possible |

## 🧭 5 Qualities Tech Companies Look For

1. **Think about others first** — What does your audience/user need?
2. **Own the outcome** — Take responsibility for the whole result
3. **Never stop learning** — Treat your syllabus as a floor, not a ceiling
4. **Be someone people can count on** — Show up, be honest, be consistent
5. **Start before you're ready** — Done and imperfect beats perfect and never started
